Team access

  • Waiting: has an account, can't see anything until you approve them.
  • Viewer: sees every order and can comment in threads.
  • Editor: also adds orders, edits fields, assigns people and moves cards.
  • Admin: everything an Editor can do, plus deleting orders.
  • Removed: account kept for history, all access blocked.
